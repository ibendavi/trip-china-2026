Act I · Gannan — living Tibetan culture and plateau hiking top ↑

Sep 2–10 · Elevated Trips with Sangye · CNY 41,500 all-in for two (lodging, all meals lunch Sep 2 → dinner Sep 10, entrances, GL8, guide/driver costs, water) · final day-by-day pending from Ben.

Wed Sep 2–Thu Sep 3 · Lanzhou → Labrang; Ganjia grasslands

Sangye meets us at the Lanzhou hotel on the morning of Sep 2 and we drive to Xiahe. Labrang Monastery, the kora and thangka wall, plus a Ganjia Basin day with the gorge hike. Two nights at altitude 2,900 m follow the low-altitude Lanzhou arrival night.

Altitude Xiahe about 2,920 m · Nights two in Xiahe

Fri Sep 4–Sat Sep 5 · Gahai → Langmusi; Namo Gorge and the nomad night

Cross the grasslands via Gahai Lake to Langmusi, Sangye's home region: the two monasteries and village time. Sep 5 is a Namo Gorge half-day hike followed by the nomad-camp overnight, the Sangye special that Elevated folded into the standard program.

Nights Langmusi + nomad camp · Open item bedding/toilet expectations and a hotel opt-out in writing

Sun Sep 6–Mon Sep 7 · Zhagana, both nights inside the basin

Two nights at the Tiancheng Gana Meiduo homestay resort inside the village basin. Sep 7 is a full flexible hiking day; Sangye picks the route with us based on weather and energy rather than forcing a fixed high trail.

Nights two inside Zhagana · Kept below 4,000 m pending insurance review

Tue Sep 8–Wed Sep 9 · Hezuo → Linxia; Bafang quarter

Leave Zhagana for Hezuo and the Milarepa Tower. The requested change from v3: continue to Linxia for the Sep 9 night and walk the Bafang quarter in the evening, instead of a second Hezuo night. This is the driving-neutral fill that replaced the rejected Tangke detour.

Night Linxia near Bafang · Status requested in the Aug 3 counter, awaiting Ben's day-by-day

Thu Sep 10 · Bingling Temple → Lanzhou West drop-off

Bingling grottoes by speedboat in the morning, then Lanzhou. Ben drops us in the evening at a hotel near Lanzhou West station and that night's hotel is included in the package. Farewell to Sangye here; Sep 11 morning needs no service, we walk to the 08:29 train ourselves.

Move GL8 + speedboat · Night Lanzhou West (included) · Open item hotel to be named in the contract
