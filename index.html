Act I · Gannan — living Tibetan culture and plateau hiking top ↑

Sep 1–11 · Elevated Trips with Sangye · signed, deposit paid · CNY 41,500 all-in for two (ten nights, all meals lunch Sep 2 → breakfast Sep 11, entrances, GL8, guide/driver costs, water) · balance due before the trip. Each day shows a solitude score: 1 = crowded Chinese-tourism site, 5 = effectively alone on the plateau. Driving distances and any night not spent in a hotel are in bold.

Wed Sep 2–Thu Sep 3 · Lanzhou → Labrang; Ganjia grasslands

Sangye meets us at the Holiday Inn Lanzhou Center on the morning of Sep 2 and we drive to Xiahe. Labrang Monastery, the kora and thangka wall, plus a Ganjia Basin day with the gorge hike. Two nights at altitude 2,900 m follow the low-altitude Lanzhou arrival night.

Altitude Xiahe about 2,920 m · Drive ~230 km Sep 2, ~80 km Ganjia loop Sep 3 · Nights two in Xiahe (hotel) · Solitude 2/5 at Labrang (pilgrims and tour groups), 4/5 in the Ganjia Basin

Fri Sep 4–Sat Sep 5 · Gahai → Langmusi; Namo Gorge and the nomad night

Cross the grasslands via Gahai Lake to Langmusi, Sangye's home region: the two monasteries and village time. Sep 5 is a Namo Gorge half-day hike followed by the nomad-camp overnight, the Sangye special that Elevated folded into the standard program.

Drive ~180 km Sep 4, local moves Sep 5 · Nights Langmusi (hotel), then the nomad-camp overnight — not a hotel (bedding/toilet expectations and a hotel opt-out still owed in writing) · Solitude 3/5 in Langmusi town, 4/5 in Namo Gorge, 5/5 at the camp

Sun Sep 6–Mon Sep 7 · Zhagana, both nights inside the basin

Two nights at the Tiancheng Gana Meiduo homestay resort inside the village basin. Sep 7 is a full flexible hiking day; Sangye picks the route with us based on weather and energy rather than forcing a fixed high trail.

Drive ~150 km Sep 6, shuttle-only Sep 7 · Nights two at the Tiancheng Gana Meiduo homestay resort — a village homestay, not a standard hotel · Solitude 2/5 at the shuttle viewpoints, 4/5 once past the first platforms on foot · Kept below 4,000 m pending insurance review

Tue Sep 8–Wed Sep 9 · Hezuo → Linxia; Bafang quarter

Leave Zhagana for Hezuo and the Milarepa Tower. The requested change from v3: continue to Linxia for the Sep 9 night and walk the Bafang quarter in the evening, instead of a second Hezuo night. This is the driving-neutral fill that replaced the rejected Tangke detour.

Drive ~200 km Zhagana→Hezuo Sep 8, ~105 km Hezuo→Linxia Sep 9 · Nights Hezuo and Linxia (hotels) · Solitude 3/5 — real towns, very few Western tourists · Status requested in the Aug 3 counter, awaiting Ben's day-by-day

Thu Sep 10 · Bingling Temple → Lanzhou West drop-off

Bingling grottoes by speedboat in the morning, then Lanzhou. Sangye drops us in the evening at a hotel near Lanzhou West station and that night's hotel is included. Breakfast Sep 11 is still on the package, and Elevated runs us to the station for the 08:29 train at no charge.

Drive ~50 km Linxia→Liujiaxia dam + speedboat, then ~80 km to Lanzhou · Night Lanzhou West (hotel, included) · Solitude 3/5 — boat groups at Bingling, quieter midweek · Open item hotel still to be named by Elevated

Act II · Hexi Corridor — Zhangye and Dunhuang top ↑

Sep 11–15 · Steven Kang, Silk Road Angel Tours · signed and stamped, deposit paid · CNY 19,040 (option B) · balance CNY 13,328 handed over in person in Zhangye on Sep 11. Same solitude scale as Act I; mileage and non-hotel nights in bold.

Fri Sep 11 · Lanzhou → Zhangye; Danxia sunset; midnight sleeper

D2743 arrives Zhangye West 11:10 and Steven takes over: Giant Buddha Temple in town, then a private tour of the Danxia Landform park's best areas plus the regular sunset viewpoint. A day-use room at the Ducheng Holiday Hotel for showers and rest, then the K9667 sleeper at 00:32 in soft-sleeper berths (two lowers requested; Steven in the same class).

Rail D2743 08:29–11:10 · K9667 00:32 → 08:05 · Drive ~40 km city ↔ Danxia · Night overnight on the K9667 soft sleeper — not a hotel (day-use room for showers before boarding) · Solitude 1/5 at Danxia's regular platforms (Gansu's busiest park; the private-tour areas closer to 3/5), 3/5 at Giant Buddha Temple · Tickets Steven buys both when sales open ~Aug 28

Sat Sep 12 · Dunhuang arrival; Mingsha dunes at dusk

Arrive 08:05 with the Grand Sun room already ours: the option-B extra night (Sep 11) guarantees immediate occupancy, shower and real rest. Late afternoon: Crescent Moon Lake, the Singing-Sand Dunes and a camel ride in the good light.

Drive ~10 km local · Night Dunhuang Grand Sun (hotel; nights Sep 11–14 booked) · Solitude 1/5 at Mingsha/Crescent at dusk — expect festival-scale crowds; the camel ride works anyway · Design deliberately light after the 19-hour Sep 11

Sun Sep 13 · Western desert circuit

Jade Gate Pass, the Han-dynasty wall remains, and Yadan National Geopark, timed for afternoon light. Jiayuguan is deliberately omitted; the day belongs to the far-western sites.

Drive ~380 km round trip, the longest driving day of the trip · Night Grand Sun (hotel) · Solitude 4/5 — the far-western sites are near-empty outside holiday weeks

Mon Sep 14 · A full Mogao day

Digital Exhibition Center films at 8:30, shuttle to the cliff, and the cave visit with the official English guide from 9:45. Three special caves are booked (CNY 400 refunded on the spot if only two are open that day). Free time on site afterward; back to the hotel by about 13:00 to relax.

Drive ~50 km round trip incl. the digital center · Night Grand Sun (hotel) · Solitude 2/5 in the standard 30-person English group, 4/5 inside the special caves (small paid groups) · Note special-cave numbers are decided by the site on the day

Tue Sep 15 · Dunhuang → Beijing

Breakfast, 8:45 checkout, Steven's transfer to the airport by 9:00, and CA9844 to Beijing at 10:40, landing 13:45. The tour ends at the terminal; the Beijing academic week begins.
